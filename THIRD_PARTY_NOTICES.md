# Stockfish

The CLI and website include Stockfish 19 through the `stockfish` npm package (Stockfish.js
19.0.1). The engine runs as a separate Node/WebAssembly process and communicates
using UCI. Stockfish and its WebAssembly engine are licensed under GPL v3;
the package includes the license in `Copying.txt`.

- Package and corresponding source: https://github.com/nmrugg/stockfish.js/tree/v19.0.1
- Upstream engine: https://github.com/official-stockfish/Stockfish

The project's MIT license does not replace the licenses of its dependencies.
